## 🎯 The Mathematical Heart

### Black-Scholes in Context

```
∂V/∂t + rS(∂V/∂S) + (1/2)σ²S²(∂²V/∂S²) - rV = 0

Applied to Ethical Code:
V = Ethical value of technology
S = Scale of beneficiaries served  
r = Social discount rate (9.5%) = inflation + social premium
σ = Market volatility (25%)
t = Time (years)
```
